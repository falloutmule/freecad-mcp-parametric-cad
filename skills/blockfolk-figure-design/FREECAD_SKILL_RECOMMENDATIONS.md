# Recommendations for the general FreeCAD skill

Reviewed installed `freecad-mcp-parametric-cad` 2.1.0.dev1, including its mechanical-evidence reference. **No general skill source was edited.** Its current router already selects evidence from the claim, requires one writer per FreeCAD process, preserves feature-scoped physical evidence, distinguishes requirements/keepouts/physical facts, and says capability flags are not native-test acceptance. The mechanical reference already separates intended contact, clearance, motion, print quality, handling strength and physical fit; treats CAD/mesh/slice/G-code/physical results as separate states; preserves hashes; and recommends the smallest useful physical article. Keep those rules. Several items below need only a short worked example or clarification, not another rule or framework.

| Topic | Recommendation | Appropriate home |
|---|---|---|
| Claim separation | A short optional mechanical-assembly checklist should name free motion, retention and load/pose resistance as independent claims. Do not add a universal joint PASS field. | General mechanical evidence reference; actual cam/leaf rules stay Blockfolk-specific |
| Capture evidence | Add an example showing why one blocked translation and collision-free rotation do not establish capture. Require explicit escape-mode coverage and final-material evidence for the claimed retention family. | General mechanical evidence; no universal socket validator or Blockfolk cap dimensions |
| Semantic configurations | Encourage a named configuration to satisfy its intended function as well as its matrix and interference check: a seated figure, open lid, withdrawn drawer, folded linkage. | Brief general assembly guidance; humanoid landmarks and exact poses remain here |
| Early product review | For appearance-sensitive products, inspect representative major views before expensive verification. Make this conditional on the brief, not a mandatory four-render gate for every washer or bracket. | General routing sentence; Blockfolk silhouette rubric stays here |
| Evidence writers and state | Existing CAD writer ownership is good. Clarify that independent verifiers also need uncontested output paths. Pin evidence to artifact hashes, resolved feature/state IDs, parameter values and configurations. Historical document names alone are insufficient. | General evidence/export guidance; do not add a second locking system |
| Slicer sanity | For fabrication handoffs, supplement mesh topology with dimensions, plausible mass/time, expected component count and source-profile identity. “Accepted by slicer” is not a mesh or physical-clearance proof. | Optional FDM reference; Aquila settings and PIP layer heights remain here |
| Physical boundaries | Preserve nominal CAD, slicer estimates, user reports and measured results as different evidence types. Supplement historical reports instead of rewriting their previous physical status. | Existing general physical-evidence reference, with one concise worked example |
| When to print | Once digital gates pass, identify which unresolved questions require a physical test instead of escalating CAD grid density indefinitely. | General fabrication handoff guidance; specific coupon geometry remains domain-specific |

Do **not** move the joint option catalog, paired curved followers, 0.45/0.35 mm starts, Blockfolk head/body ratios, canted shoulders, Aquila profile or humanoid visual checks into the general skill. They would bloat a broad CAD workflow and suggest unsupported universality. A domain skill should own those details and link to existing infrastructure.

A small general improvement should be justified by multiple mechanical-product use cases and tested independently. This task's fixture suite demonstrates a useful domain checker, not acceptance of every general helper or a reason to build a new verification framework.

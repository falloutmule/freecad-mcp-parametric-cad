# Mechanical evidence and release

## Authority and isolation

Digital provenance identifies upstream repository/ref, exact archive/file/object, SHA-256, license, role and limitations. Authenticate membership, not filename proximity. Preflight checks payload integrity, not source authority or safety of executing untrusted CAD/scripts.

Physical evidence identifies article, exercised feature, datums, method and uncertainty. Propagate uncertainty into interfaces and allowances. Real hardware and exercised articles outrank nominal CAD only for features supported by adequate evidence. Uncalibrated photographs may identify a face, not dimensions or lateral handedness.

Do not synthesize missing hardware from generic machine knowledge. Separate authenticated geometry, physical correlation, reference-only screens, constructed keepouts and unsupported estimates. A tool cylinder is not authenticated hardware. Reference screens need applicable authority before becoming blocking requirements.

For repository work, record actual full HEAD plus staged and unstaged state. Enforce a requested expected-HEAD gate without changing HEAD. Investigate using scratch copies outside Git when isolation is required. Before an authorized commit, inspect the proposed tree: do not include, unstage, overwrite or discard unrelated work.

## Fit and structural evidence

Separate intended contact, clearance, service access, motion, print quality, handling strength and physical fit. Zero intersection does not establish manufacturing margin or stiffness. A failed 0.25 mm ligament proves that failure case, not 0.26 mm success or a universal 5 mm minimum.

Near fasteners, distinguish deliberate access openings from raised structures entirely outside the access envelope. Quantify relevant hole-to-edge/window relationships. Avoid accidental near-tangent bridges, partial holes and slivers; do not invent strength thresholds.

Declare contact intent before measuring. Pair touching does not prove footprint, normal, engagement or capacity. Freeze requirements/tolerances before evaluation; never alter them to manufacture PASS.

Map external-verifier requirements to demonstrated operators of the pinned release. Unsupported semantics remain named construction assertions, justified alternative evidence or physical checks. Candidate files must not mutate approved bundles. Do not expand verifier v0.2 during ordinary design. Without an independent verifier, disclose native-check scope rather than inventing independence or automatically blocking CAD.

## Physical gates and fabrication

Use the smallest useful article for the next unknown. Preserve independently exercised PASS facts and change only failed/coupled features. Record waivers as `PHYSICAL TEST WAIVED BY OWNER`, never PASS; identify surviving authority for dependent datums. Waivers cannot supply missing physical dimensions.

Required uncertainty blocks dependent release, not independent work. Diagnose source, frame, architecture, parameter, export, process and physical-interference errors separately. Repair uncertainty through evidence/tool recovery, not geometry tuning.

CAD, mesh, slice, G-code and physical results are separate states. Record the print transform and effective machine, nozzle, material, temperatures, volume, layers, walls, top/bottom, infill, supports, extrusion mode and start/end G-code. Mechanical donor and printer are distinct identities.

G-code checks prove only supported command properties: scale, extrusion/bed bounds, layers, temperatures, modes and blocking warnings. Informational warnings do not automatically block printing; missing filament metadata is not zero material. Geometry/orientation/tessellation/profile changes invalidate downstream artifacts.

`READY FOR PHYSICAL TEST` requires safe-fabrication digital prerequisites to pass or be explicitly waived without false promotion. Never upload, queue or start a print without authorization. Preserve exact hashes, scoped physical results, failures and remaining production gates.
